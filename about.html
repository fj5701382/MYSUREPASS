MySurePass is Nigeria's precision technology-driven learning support hub. We build smart, highly accessible tools for mock exam dry runs and full exam preparation commitments, confident, and stress-free.

Our Mission

To make quality exam preparation accessible to every student, regardless of their background. We provide comprehensive past questions, state-of-the-art practice tools, and curated learning resources.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading education technology platform, transforming how millions of students prepare for exams through personalised deep learning analytics, past-exam practice sets, and exemplary e-commerce platforms.

ATTRACTIVE FEATURES

What We Offer

We provide tools designed specifically to enable the current West African education system and help make education and testing environments better for every learner.

Past Questions Bank

Over a thousand well-set, highly dedicated past questions across all examinations for WAEC, JAMB, NECO, and NABTEB examinations.

CBT Practice Mode

Provides a simulated real exam experience, using real multiple-choice questions from leading comprehensive solutions with proven questions, solutions and explanations for students.

Teacher Tools

Empowers educators with custom testing configurations, class divisions, and optimal arrangement of past questions for their learners and school populations.

API Access

THE STORY

Our Story

MySurePass was founded to address a critical challenge in Nigeria's secondary education ecosystem — the fragmentation of quality exam prep resources. For years, students had trouble locating validated, curated, and comprehensive materials to help them study and perform their best. We gathered dedicated lecturers, expert curriculum analysts, and senior technology wizards to build a living, breathing digital platform that enables students to study smarter, break down complex concepts, and secure academic success.
