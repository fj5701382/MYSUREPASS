HOW IT WORKS

From sign-up to results in three steps

This is the flow we're building toward.

  1. Create your account

    Sign up as a teacher with your email, phone number and school. It takes about a minute.

  2. Build your exam

    Add questions from your bank, choose the time limit and decide how questions are shuffled.

  3. Share and review

    Send it to your class, then check scores and the questions that tripped students up.
