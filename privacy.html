In short: we collect only what we need to run your account and improve your practice experience. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

MySurePass Technologies ("MySurePass", "we", "us") operates the MySurePass website and related services, a digital platform for Nigerian students preparing for exams such as WAEC, JAMB, NECO and NABTEB. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and other applicable Nigerian law.

2. Data we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, username and password (stored in hashed form).
  • Learning activity: exams and subjects you select, practice sessions, answers, scores, time spent and progress.
  • Device and usage data: browser type, device type, IP address, pages visited and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you contact support.
  • Sign-in providers: if you sign in with Google or Apple, basic profile information those providers share with us.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and verify your email or phone number.
  • To provide practice questions, CBT sessions and progress tracking.
  • To personalise recommendations and show you relevant subjects.
  • To respond to support requests and send service messages such as verification codes and password resets.
  • To detect abuse, prevent fraud and keep the platform secure.
  • To understand how the platform is used so we can improve it.

4. Lawful basis

Under the NDPA we process your data only where we have a lawful basis. Depending on the activity, this is: performance of our agreement with you (providing your account and services), your consent (for example, optional marketing messages or non-essential cookies), our legitimate interests (securing and improving the platform, where these do not override your rights), or compliance with a legal obligation.

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting processing that took place before withdrawal.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the platform, such as hosting, analytics, email and SMS delivery, under contracts that require them to protect your data.
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.
  • A successor organisation, if MySurePass is involved in a merger or acquisition, with notice to you.

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used.

  • Essential cookies: needed for sign-in, security and core features. These cannot be switched off.
  • Preference cookies: remember settings such as your remembered login.
  • Analytics cookies: help us see which pages and features are used. Where required, we ask for your consent first.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site may not work properly without essential cookies.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Account and learning data is kept while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep certain records to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords and access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong, unique password and never share your verification codes.

If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used and request access to it;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • ask us to delete your data, where the law allows;
  • object to or ask us to restrict certain processing, including direct marketing;
  • request a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time; and
  • lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 13. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Children

Many of our users are students under 18. If you are under 18, please use MySurePass with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. Where the law requires parental or guardian consent to process a child's data, we will seek it. Parents and guardians can contact us at any time to review or delete a child's data.

11. International transfers

Some of our service providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your data receives a level of protection consistent with the NDPA, for example through contractual safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or in the app.

13. Contact us

Questions or requests about your data? Reach us through our support page or email workspace19441@gmail.com.
